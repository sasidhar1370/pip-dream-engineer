Chapter I

The Spark in the Stardust

Before Pip learned to steer a cloud, he was just a stray star-spark caught in the curtains of the Midnight Nursery. The Elder Weavers noticed how he could soothe the crankiest newborns simply by whispering lullabies in harmony with the ticking clock. They handed him his first spool of silver stardust yarn and said: “A dream isn’t just asleep, Pip. It’s a pocket universe where fear forgets its own name.” He’s been weaving ever since.

Location: Nursery Cradle No. 1 Year 001 · First Spark
Chapter II
🌙

Apprenticeship of the Moonbeams

Training under the Moonbeam Guild wasn’t easy. Pip had to master the delicate art of Cloud-Fluffing at 30,000 feet, where a single sneeze could turn a fluffy dream into a drizzle of spilled warm milk. He spent six moon cycles calibrating nocturnal lanterns, learning which wavelengths of amber keep monsters tucked inside closets. His mentor, Master Barnaby, granted him the Brass Nightcap Bell when Pip successfully guided three hundred restless cradles into deep REM sleep in a single shift.

Rank: Apprentice Weaver Year 002 · The Great Calibration
Chapter III
☁️

First Flight on Cloud 9

On the night of the Great Blue Moon, Pip received his solo commission: deliver twenty-four custom dreamscapes across the highest gables of Midnight Nursery before dawn. With his dream lantern glowing soft peach and the lullaby box humming in his satchel, he took off into the velvet indigo sky. Every nursery chimney greeted him like an old friend. When the morning sun crept in, twenty-four peaceful toddlers woke up smiling from adventures they would never quite forget.
